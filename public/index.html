How winners are chosen

Built on evidence, not popularity

Every finalist passes through the same three stages. Public enthusiasm counts, but expert review against strict merit rubrics decides most of the verdict.

1

Open nominations

A six-week submission window through a secure online portal.

2

Evidence-based review

Nominees submit portfolios, impact data and professional references.

3

The weighted verdict

Jury scores and the public vote combine into one final score per category.

80% jury · 20% public

An expert jury scores each finalist on impact, leadership, innovation, community contribution and the quality of their evidence. The public vote adds the community's voice.

Expert jury evaluationPublic choice vote

One vote per person in each category, with encrypted data handling to prevent vote manipulation.
