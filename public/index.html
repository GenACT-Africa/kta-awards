33 categories across five core economic pillars

Education is the focus area of the 2026 edition. Open a category to see who qualifies and the evidence required.

Education (9)

Focus area
  • Educator of the Year

    Who qualifies: Kenyan teacher or lecturer teaching in a Tanzanian school or college, with at least 3 years in service.

    Evidence required: Learner outcomes, mentorship record, two professional references.

  • Higher Education Leadership

    Who qualifies: Kenyan vice-chancellor, dean, head of department or academic leader at a Tanzanian university or college.

    Evidence required: Programmes launched, enrolment or research growth, institutional impact.

  • School Leadership & Institutional Management

    Who qualifies: Kenyan school principals, headteachers and directors managing top-performing institutions in Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Exam results, inspection or accreditation reports, enrolment trends, governance record.

  • STEM & Vocational Education (TVET) Excellence

    Who qualifies: Outstanding educators in science, technology, engineering, mathematics and technical or hands-on skills training.

    Evidence required: Graduate outcomes, employer partnerships, workshop or lab innovations.

  • Early Childhood Development (ECD) Pioneer

    Who qualifies: Teachers, tutors and founders excelling in early childhood care, pre-school education and foundational learning.

    Evidence required: Child development progress, licensing, parent testimonials, teaching methods.

  • EdTech Innovation

    Who qualifies: Founder or team deploying digital learning tools in Tanzanian classrooms or training settings.

    Evidence required: Users reached, learning-impact data, product demo or portfolio.

  • Research & Scholarly Publication Award

    Who qualifies: University academics and researchers whose published work contributes to policy, regional integration or cross-border development.

    Evidence required: Publication list, citations or policy uptake, institutional reference.

  • Student Mentorship & Youth Guidance Award

    Who qualifies: Teachers and counsellors who go beyond the curriculum with career counselling, sports coaching and youth leadership training.

    Evidence required: Mentee outcomes, programme descriptions, student and parent testimonials.

  • Community Inclusion

    Who qualifies: Individual or organisation widening access for girls, learners with disabilities or rural communities.

    Evidence required: Beneficiary numbers, partnerships, community testimonials.

Corporate & Formal (10)

  • Executive Leaders

    Who qualifies: Kenyan CEO, MD or C-suite leader of a company operating in Tanzania for 3+ years.

    Evidence required: Revenue or jobs growth, governance record, leadership references.

  • Healthcare

    Who qualifies: Clinicians, administrators and health-enterprise leaders serving Tanzanian patients.

    Evidence required: Licences, patient-outcome or service improvements, peer references.

  • Finance & Banking

    Who qualifies: Bankers, fintech, insurance and investment professionals active in Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Products launched, financial-inclusion reach, portfolio or market growth.

  • Legal & Governance Luminaries

    Who qualifies: Licensed advocates, compliance officers and board leaders practising in Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Admission or licence proof, notable matters, ethics and standing record.

  • Engineering, Energy & Infrastructure Leader

    Who qualifies: Civil engineers, project directors and energy specialists leading major infrastructure, construction and power projects.

    Evidence required: Project scale and delivery record, safety and compliance, professional registration.

  • Telecoms & Technology Executive

    Who qualifies: Technology heads, software architects and digital transformation leaders driving enterprise IT and mobile financial services.

    Evidence required: Systems delivered, users or transactions served, innovation case study.

  • Human Capital & Organizational Culture Champion

    Who qualifies: HR directors and talent strategists excelling in workforce development, labour compliance and cross-border team integration.

    Evidence required: Training and retention results, compliance record, team-integration initiatives.

  • Supply Chain & Procurement Professional of the Year

    Who qualifies: Logistics directors, procurement heads and supply chain managers optimising regional trade corridors.

    Evidence required: Cost or lead-time savings, corridor performance, procurement integrity record.

  • Insurance, Risk & Actuarial Excellence

    Who qualifies: Leaders in underwriting, risk management and insurance services navigating complex commercial environments.

    Evidence required: Portfolio performance, risk or claims outcomes, professional qualifications.

  • Corporate Sustainability & ESG Leader

    Who qualifies: Corporate managers driving environmental, social and governance initiatives within multinational or regional firms in Tanzania.

    Evidence required: ESG programme results, reporting or certifications, community impact.

Agriculture (2)

  • Agri-Industrialist of the Year

    Who qualifies: Kenyan-led farming, agro-processing or agri-value-chain business operating in Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Yield or processing volumes, export reach, jobs and out-grower impact.

  • Sustainable & Climate Champions

    Who qualifies: Individuals or firms leading renewable energy, conservation or climate-smart initiatives.

    Evidence required: Measurable emissions, ecosystem or resource-saving results, project reports.

Non-Formal & SME (8)

  • Micro-Enterprise Innovation

    Who qualifies: Small or informal Kenyan business with 1+ year trading and a creative solution.

    Evidence required: Revenue growth, customer base, photos or product samples.

  • Hospitality

    Who qualifies: Hotels, restaurants and tourism operators run by Kenyans in Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Service standards, guest ratings, local employment figures.

  • Manufacturing & Logistics

    Who qualifies: Factories, assembly, freight and supply-chain operators serving regional trade.

    Evidence required: Output, efficiency gains, cross-border trade volumes.

  • Cross-Border Trade & Merchant Award

    Who qualifies: Small-to-medium traders and merchants who move goods, agricultural produce or services between Kenya and Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Trade records or invoices, years trading, licences, supplier or customer references.

  • Women-Led SME / Female Entrepreneur of the Year

    Who qualifies: Women founding or running small businesses, retail shops or informal trade enterprises across Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Revenue or customer growth, jobs created, photos, references.

  • Tech-Enabled SME / Digital Retail Award

    Who qualifies: Small business owners using e-commerce, social selling (WhatsApp, Instagram) or digital payments to grow their customer base.

    Evidence required: Online sales or customer growth, platform screenshots, payment data.

  • Youth SME Founder Award

    Who qualifies: Young entrepreneurs under 35 building viable micro-businesses, agencies or startups in the region.

    Evidence required: Proof of age, business registration, traction metrics, portfolio.

  • Culinary & Food Services SME Award

    Who qualifies: Kenyan-owned restaurants, caterers and food vendors introducing Kenyan cuisine and hospitality to Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Menu and customer reviews, food-safety licences, sales growth.

Impact & Special (4)

  • Diaspora Champions

    Who qualifies: Kenyans in Tanzania who mentor, volunteer or lead community programmes.

    Evidence required: Programme reach, volunteer hours, community references.

  • Media Pioneers

    Who qualifies: Journalists, broadcasters and digital creators covering East African stories.

    Evidence required: Body of work, audience reach, ethical standards record.

  • Sports Icons

    Who qualifies: Athletes, coaches and administrators advancing sport in Tanzania.

    Evidence required: Achievements, youth-development work, endorsements.

  • Lifetime Legacy Award

    Who qualifies: Kenyans with 25+ years of sustained impact in Tanzania, nominated by peers or institutions.

    Evidence required: Career summary, impact evidence, three referees.

A standard of uncompromising integrity

Every finalist passes through the same three stages.

Step 1

Open nominations

A six-week submission window via secure online portals.

Step 2

Evidence-based review

Portfolios, impact data and professional references are required.

Step 3

The weighted verdict

80% Expert jury (strict rubrics)20% Public choice vote

Security: one ballot per mobile number, with encrypted data handling to protect transparency and prevent vote manipulation.
